# Dashboard Plan

Use `data/cleaned/cleaned_retail_sales.csv` as the main table in Power BI or Tableau.

## Page 1: Executive Overview

KPI cards:

- Revenue
- Profit
- Profit margin
- Orders
- Units sold

Charts:

- Monthly revenue and profit line chart
- Revenue and profit by category
- Profit by region

Filters:

- Month
- Region
- Category
- Segment

## Page 2: Product Performance

Charts:

- Top 10 products by revenue
- Product profit margin
- Units sold by product
- Discount rate by product

Business use:

- Identify high-sales products that may be hurting profit.
- Find products that deserve more marketing support.

## Page 3: Discount and Margin Review

Charts:

- Profit margin by discount band
- Revenue by discount band
- Discount rate by category
- Returned orders by category

Business use:

- Decide where discounts should be reduced, targeted, or removed.
